Our story

A tradition flowing through every bottle

Our love for the Slavonian land and the vine has been passed down through generations of the Soldo family. Our vineyards lie on the sun-drenched slopes of the Kutjevo wine region, where mineral-rich soil and abundant sunshine create the perfect conditions for grapes of exceptional quality.

Every bottle that leaves our cellar carries the mark of dedicated work, patience, and deep respect for the tradition of Golden Slavonia — with a touch of modern winemaking know-how.

Why Soldo

From grape to perfection in the glass

We make no compromises when it comes to quality. Every bottle bearing the Soldo signature is the result of careful hand-harvesting, a deep understanding of our land, and an immeasurable love of winemaking. We believe wine is made in the vineyard. We've learned to listen to nature, combining the gifts of Slavonian soil with dedicated family labor. The result is wines with authentic character that we pour with pride.
