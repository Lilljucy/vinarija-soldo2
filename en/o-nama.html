Our story

Meet Vinarija Soldo

Three generations, one passion. Discover the family journey and the love of winemaking woven into every bottle we produce.

For as long as we can remember

A family rooted in the land

The story of Vinarija Soldo began long ago, when Gojko Soldo's father first planted vines on the fertile slopes of our region. What began as a small family vineyard is today a winery that nurtures the same spirit — hands-on work, respect for nature, and the patience wine deserves.

Today the winery is run by the third generation of the family, who combine traditional methods with modern winemaking know-how so that every bottle carries the authentic taste of our region.

A journey through time

Our history

1989

The first vineyard

Gojko Soldo's father planted the first vines on the family estate, laying a solid foundation for our future winery.

2000

Expanding the family estate

Gojko Soldo expands the vineyards and dedicates himself fully to viticulture, taking the care of the vines to a new, more serious level.

2011

The first bottle and wine cellar

After years of dedicated work in the vineyard, the cellar was built and the family proudly presented the first bottle bearing the Soldo signature.

Today

The third generation continues the story

With the expert signature of winemaker Mirko Soldo, we combine family tradition with modern winemaking. Today we happily welcome guests to our tasting room, with premium wines and a view of the slopes of the Kutjevo wine region.
