Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your data when you visit our website.

Last updated: August 17, 2026

1. Data Controller

The controller of personal data collected through this website (vinarija-soldo.hr) is Vinarija Soldo, Majdanska ul. 53, 34335 Vetovo, Croatia. For any privacy-related questions, contact us at soldovina@gmail.com or by phone at +385 98 954 4621.

2. What Data We Collect

This website does not contain data-collection forms or visitor-tracking tools (no Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or similar systems). We collect personal data only when you voluntarily send it to us — for example via a WhatsApp message, email, or phone call, when you contact us with an inquiry, a tasting reservation, or a wine order.

Data received this way (e.g. name, phone number, email address, message content) is used solely to respond to your inquiry and is not shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

The website uses the browser's local storage (localStorage) for two functional purposes:

  • Age verification — remembers your confirmation that you are 18 or older, for 30 minutes, so the question isn't asked again on every page refresh.
  • Cookie preference — remembers whether you accepted or declined the cookie notice, so the banner isn't shown again.

This data stays exclusively on your device, is not sent to our servers, and is not used for tracking or analytics.

4. Third-Party Services

Certain pages embed third-party content that may set its own cookies in accordance with its own privacy policy:

  • Google Maps — the Contact page displays our location via an embedded Google Maps frame.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) — contact buttons link to WhatsApp, a service of Meta Platforms Ireland Limited.
  • Instagram and Facebook — links to our social media profiles lead to platforms operated by Meta.

We recommend reviewing those services' own privacy policies for details on how they process data on their end.

5. Your Rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal data you have provided to us, and to lodge a complaint with the Croatian Personal Data Protection Agency (AZOP) if you believe your rights have been violated. To exercise your rights, contact us at soldovina@gmail.com.

6. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to update this privacy policy from time to time. The date of the last revision is shown at the top of this page.
